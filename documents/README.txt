Put your final planning PDF documents in this folder.

Each document link in index.html points to a file here, e.g.:
  documents/design-and-access-statement.pdf

Either:
- Name your PDFs to match the existing links in index.html, or
- Rename the links in index.html (search for "documents/") to match your actual filenames.

Add or remove <a class="doc-card"> blocks in the "Documents & Downloads" section
of index.html if your list of documents differs from the placeholder list.
